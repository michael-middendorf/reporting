Methodology & Scoring

Points are computed entirely from raw Virtuous activity — contact notes and events authored by the Mission Advancement team. The dashboard reads only raw data and does all scoring in the browser. Scoring follows the official MAS Points comp-plan rubric; where the working spreadsheet diverges, the difference is flagged below.
